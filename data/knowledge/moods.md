## happy
Happy: bright, major-key, high valence. Good for: celebrating, mornings, boosting mood.

## chill
Chill: calm, steady, low stress. Good for: studying, background listening, winding down.

## intense
Intense: loud, fast, driving. Good for: workouts, gaming, pushing through hard tasks.

## relaxed
Relaxed: slow and gentle, often acoustic. Good for: coffee, reading, evenings.

## moody
Moody: darker, minor-key, introspective. Good for: night drives, reflective moments.

## focused
Focused: steady, minimal vocals, repetitive patterns. Good for: deep work, studying, coding.
