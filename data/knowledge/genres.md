## lofi
Lo-fi hip-hop: soft drums, vinyl crackle, mellow jazz chords. Common uses: studying, focus, late-night coding. Typical energy 0.3-0.5. Related: ambient, jazz, chillhop.

## pop
Pop: catchy hooks, verse-chorus structure, polished production. Common uses: parties, workouts, road trips. Typical energy 0.6-0.95. Related: indie pop, edm, dance.

## indie pop
Indie pop: pop melodies with a lighter, guitar-driven or DIY sound. Common uses: sunny days, casual listening. Typical energy 0.5-0.8. Related: pop, folk.

## rock
Rock: electric guitars, live drums, driving rhythm. Common uses: workouts, venting, intense focus. Typical energy 0.7-1.0. Related: edm (for intensity), hip-hop.

## ambient
Ambient: slow textures, little or no percussion, spacious sound. Common uses: sleep, meditation, deep focus. Typical energy 0.1-0.35. Related: lofi, folk, jazz.

## jazz
Jazz: swing or laid-back grooves, improvisation, acoustic instruments. Common uses: coffee shops, dinner, relaxing. Typical energy 0.2-0.5. Related: soul, lofi, r&b.

## synthwave
Synthwave: 1980s-inspired synthesizers and drum machines. Common uses: night drives, gaming, moody focus. Typical energy 0.6-0.8. Related: edm, pop.

## hip-hop
Hip-hop: rhythm-first beats with rap vocals. Common uses: workouts, commuting, hanging out. Typical energy 0.5-0.95. Related: r&b, lofi.

## r&b
R&B: smooth vocals, groove-based rhythm, soulful harmony. Common uses: relaxing evenings, date nights. Typical energy 0.4-0.6. Related: soul, hip-hop, jazz.

## edm
EDM (electronic dance music): synths, heavy bass drops, four-on-the-floor kicks. Common uses: parties, running, high-intensity workouts. Typical energy 0.85-1.0. Related: pop, synthwave.

## soul
Soul: warm vocals, gospel-rooted harmony, live band feel. Common uses: feel-good mornings, cooking. Typical energy 0.4-0.7. Related: r&b, jazz.

## reggae
Reggae: offbeat guitar skank, relaxed groove, warm bass. Common uses: beach days, chilling outside. Typical energy 0.4-0.6. Related: hip-hop, soul.

## folk
Folk: acoustic guitar, storytelling vocals, sparse arrangements. Common uses: quiet evenings, reading, unwinding. Typical energy 0.1-0.4. Related: indie pop, ambient.
